About the application

content-factory is a local internal production and channel management tool used only by the single operator of the YouTube channel 각다귀. It supports the operator’s own uploads, scheduled publication, metadata updates, playlists, and analytics queries.

The production pipeline prepares reviewed packages. Uploading and scheduling are manually authorized and executed by the operator. OAuth credentials and operation records remain on the operator’s Mac. There is no public registration or multi-user service.
